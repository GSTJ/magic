<MagicMark />

magic is the monorepo for lint, format, TypeScript, docs, observability and CI. The
packages carry the configs. `.github/workflows` carries the reusable CI jobs and the
composite actions they are built from. `default.json` carries the Renovate policy.

Repos consume all three by re-export and by tag, so a version bump is the whole upgrade.
